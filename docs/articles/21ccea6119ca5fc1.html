Mitigation

Mitigation KMW recommends connecting surveillance equipment on a separate network, allow only specific devices access to the internet, check for firmware updates regularly, and use cloud connections responsibly. Additional mitigation guidance and recommended practices are publicly available on the ICS webpage at cisa.gov/ics in the technical information paper, ICS-TIP-12-146-01B--Targeted Cyber Intrusion Detection and Mitigation Strategies.
