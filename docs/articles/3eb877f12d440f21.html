Impact

Election officials, candidates, and political organizations may be attractive targets because of their access to voter registration data, current government officials, and sensitive campaign information. Malicious cyber actors may use phishing and smishing techniques to steal login credentials and other sensitive information or install malware on targeted devices.

Assessed severity: HIGH.

Mitigation

CISA and the FBI urge election officials, campaign personnel, and the public to review the PSA, follow recommended cybersecurity practices, and remain vigilant for suspicious emails and mobile messages.

This reflects continued use of phishing as an initial-access path in broader intrusion chains.
