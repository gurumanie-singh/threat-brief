Overview

The chief technology officer and co-founder of ransomwarehelp.com , a six year old Florida business that claimed to use advanced cryptographic tools to restore data locked by ransomware, has been charged with wire fraud after an FBI sting operation found the company was simply paying the attackers for a decryption key and charging victims a markup for work that was never performed.

Mitigation

The website RansomwareHelp urged victims Do not pay the ransom!, claiming its advanced cryptographic tools could restore 100 percent of data, servers, and systems to their original format, and that there was no need to give in to extortion demands. Ihme said RansomwareHelp sent a proposal to the victim with a recovery price of $438,880, which it claimed included professional configuration of forensic servers, expert engineering hours of programming to configure decryption algorithms, and eight (6-hour processing cycles) related to the decryption process. But Ihme said a review of the company s network at the time found only one copy of the ransom note, which was unearthed from a backup snapshot on one of the infected computers.

This aligns with ongoing ransomware campaigns targeting operational continuity and recovery readiness.
